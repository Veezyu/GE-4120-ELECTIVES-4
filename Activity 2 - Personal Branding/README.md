# Activity 2: Personal Branding

## Activity Description
This activity explored how to present a consistent personal identity online by identifying personal strengths, interests, values, and goals and communicating them through digital platforms.

## What Have I Learned?
- I learned that personal branding is about communicating who I am, what I value, and what I can contribute. A consistent profile, thoughtful content, and appropriate online behavior can help create a positive digital impression.
- One challenge was choosing which personal qualities and experiences best represented me. I worked through this by reflecting on my interests, strengths, and goals and selecting details that felt genuine and relevant.
- I can apply this learning when building my professional identity as a nursing student and preparing for future employment. I can improve my personal brand by keeping my online profiles updated and sharing work that reflects my skills and growth.
