# Activity 4: Google Sheets Presentation

## Activity Description
This activity introduced the use of Google Sheets to organize, manage, and present information in a spreadsheet. It covered practical ways to structure data and communicate results clearly.

## What Have I Learned?
- I learned how spreadsheets can organize information into rows and columns and make data easier to review. I also gained experience with spreadsheet formatting and presenting information in a structured way.
- One challenge was keeping the data organized and making the spreadsheet easy to understand. I addressed this by arranging related information consistently, using clear headings, and reviewing the entries for errors.
- I can apply these skills to tracking tasks, organizing school information, and presenting simple data in future academic or professional work. I can improve by practicing formulas, sorting, filtering, and charts in Google Sheets.
